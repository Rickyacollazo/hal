HAL

A private, personal AI assistant

HAL is a personal assistant that Ricardo Collazo runs on his own computers. It is not a public product: it has a single user, and nobody else can sign up for it or use it.

With his permission, HAL connects to his own Google account (Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts) so it can carry out tasks he asks for, such as finding and organizing email, checking his calendar, or locating a file.

How HAL handles that data is described in the privacy policy.
