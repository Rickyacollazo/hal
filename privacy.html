HAL privacy policy

Last updated October 2, 2026

HAL is a personal assistant that Ricardo Collazo runs on his own computers for his own use. It has one user, him. This policy explains what HAL does with data from his Google account.

What HAL accesses

Only with the account owner's explicit consent, HAL can access his Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts. It reads, searches, organizes, drafts and, when he asks, sends or changes items in these services.

How the data is used

  • Only to carry out tasks the account owner requests.
  • Processed on his own computers. The AI model that powers HAL runs on hardware he owns.
  • Never sold, rented or shared with anyone, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models.
  • HAL's sign-in tokens are stored only on his computer, readable only by his own user account.

Google API Services User Data Policy

HAL's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

HAL keeps only what a task needs. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately stops HAL from reaching the account.
